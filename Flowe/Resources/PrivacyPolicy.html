Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

The short version. Flowe connects Pilates students with instructors. We collect only what the app needs to do that. Some of it lives in your Apple iCloud and some on Flowe's own server so features like bookings, events and reviews work between two people — but your direct messages and instructor client notes stay end-to-end encrypted, so even we can't read them. We don't sell your data and we don't process payments for sessions. You can delete your account and its data from inside the app at any time.

Who we are

Flowe ("we", "us") operates the Flowe iOS app. If you have any question about this policy or your data, contact us at pilatesflowe@gmail.com.

How you sign in

Flowe uses Sign in with Apple as its only sign-in method. Apple gives us a stable, app-specific identifier for your account and, the first time you sign in, the name you choose to share. We never receive your Apple password. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email" option, we only ever see the relay address, never your real one.

What we collect, and why

  • Your profile — the display name from Apple, and, if you teach, the listing you create: your studio's exact address and precise map location, bio, session rate, certifications you declare, specialties, lesson types and photos you upload. Instructor listings — including the exact studio address and location — are public inside the app so signed-in students can find and reach you. People browsing without an account see only your general area and distance, not the exact address.
  • Activity you create — booking requests and their status, messages, community posts, event registrations and reviews. This is the content that makes the app work between two people.
  • Location. A student who taps "Use my location" has it used on their own device to show how far instructors are — it is never uploaded or stored. An instructor who publishes a teaching location shares their exact studio address and precise coordinates, which are stored on their public listing and shown to signed-in students (and plotted on the map). If you don't want your exact address to be public, don't publish a studio location.
  • Presence (optional). If you turn on the "active / last seen" indicator, we store when you were last active so people you message can see it. It is off by default, only visible to people you can also see, and you can turn it off any time.
  • Instructor client notes (health-related). If you teach, you can keep private notes about your own clients that may include health details you record for safety — such as injuries, pregnancy or medical conditions. These notes are end-to-end encrypted: stored as ciphertext only your device can read, never shown to the student or any other user, and never used for advertising. Only you, the instructor who wrote them, can read them.
  • Photos (optional) — profile, certificate, community, event and lesson-type images you choose to add. Access is limited to the photos you pick.
  • Notifications (optional) — if you allow them, a device push token so we can alert you about a request, a reply or a session.

Where your data lives

Flowe keeps your data in two places:

  • Apple iCloud (CloudKit). Your instructor listing, community posts, events and lesson types, and your own cached activity are stored in Apple's iCloud. Instructor listings — including the exact studio address — are readable by other signed-in Flowe users so students can find and book you.
  • Flowe's own server. To connect two people securely — and to make sure only the right people can see a booking, a class roster or who is attending an event — Flowe runs its own small backend (hosted on Cloudflare, in the EU). It stores your booking requests and their status, event registrations, the reviews you write, your display name, your optional "active / last seen" presence, and, if you enable notifications, your device's push token. This server-side data is readable by Flowe, the operator, in order to run these features — it is not end-to-end encrypted.

Direct messages and instructor client notes are the exception: both are end-to-end encrypted. Flowe cannot read them — only the participants (for messages), or the instructor who wrote them (for client notes), can.

We store display names, not email addresses, on shared and server records. Even so, treat anything you post or send as visible to other users of the app; this is the strongest reason we ask you not to share sensitive personal details in messages or posts.

Who processes your data

We keep the list of companies that touch your data short, and they act only for us, under contract:

  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, iCloud storage, and Apple Push Notification service.
  • Cloudflare — hosts Flowe's backend and database (in the EU). Cloudflare processes this data only to run the service for us, not for its own purposes.

We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Payments

Flowe does not process payments for sessions. You arrange payment with your instructor directly, outside the app. The only charge Flowe collects is the optional instructor visibility subscription, which is billed by Apple through your App Store account — we never see your card details.

How we use it

To operate the app: match students and instructors, deliver bookings and messages, decide who is allowed to see each booking or roster, show the community and events, and send the notifications you opted into. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Safety and moderation

You can block another user and report content that breaks our guidelines. Reports are stored so we can review them and are visible only to us — never to the person reported. We screen public listing and post text with an automated filter.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, the data you created is removed from Apple's iCloud and from Flowe's server, except where we must keep a limited record to meet a legal obligation or resolve a dispute. Aggregate ratings — with no name attached — may remain on an instructor's profile.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account. Settings → Delete Account removes your account and erases the data you created — from both Apple's iCloud and Flowe's own server (booking requests, event registrations, reviews you wrote, your profile, presence and device tokens). Reviews others wrote and content other people left belong to them and remain.
  • Location, photos, presence and notifications are each opt-in and can be turned off any time in the app or in iOS Settings.
  • To ask a question about your data, email pilatesflowe@gmail.com.

Children

Flowe is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We'll update this policy as the app evolves and revise the date above. Significant changes will be surfaced in the app.

This policy describes Flowe's data practices in plain language and is provided for transparency. It is not legal advice.
