Help & Support

Answers to the common questions — and how to reach a human.

Booking & sessions

How do I book a session?

Open an instructor's profile from Discover, tap Book a session, pick a day and time they've made available, choose a lesson type, and send the request. It stays Pending until the instructor accepts it.

How do I pay?

Flowe doesn't handle session payments. You arrange payment with your instructor directly — the price shown is what they charge, settled between the two of you. The only thing Flowe bills is the optional instructor subscription, through Apple.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes. Open the booking in the Bookings tab and tap Cancel. Your instructor is notified.

Teaching on Flowe

How do I become an instructor?

Choose "I'm here to teach" when you sign up. You'll get a listing to fill in — bio, rate, specialties, certifications and lesson types — plus a subscription that makes you discoverable to students.

How do I set the days and hours I teach?

Dashboard → Add availability. Turn on a day, then pick the hours you teach. Students can only request the times you choose. Close a day and it stays closed until you reopen it.

What is capacity on a lesson type?

It's the maximum group size for that lesson — shown to students as a guide (for example "Up to 6"). Flowe doesn't track live spots; every booking is a direct request to you.

Privacy & safety

Who can see my messages?

Messages are delivered through the app's shared store, so treat them as visible to other signed-in users and never send sensitive details like payment or ID information.

How do I block or report someone?

Open their profile or a post, tap the menu (•••), and choose Block or Report. Blocking hides them from you; reports come to us privately.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete Account. This removes your account and the data you created. It can't be undone.
