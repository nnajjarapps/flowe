Community Guidelines

Last updated: August 2026

Flowe has zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive behaviour. Flowe is a community of Pilates students and instructors. To keep it safe, everyone who posts, messages, or reviews on Flowe agrees to these guidelines. Break them and your content will be removed and your account may be suspended or permanently removed.

Be respectful

Treat other students and instructors the way you'd want to be treated in a studio. Disagreement is fine; cruelty, harassment, and intimidation are not.

What is not allowed

There is no tolerance for any of the following, anywhere on Flowe — community discussions, event and circle chats, posts, reviews, messages, profiles, or photos:

  • Harassment or bullying — targeting, threatening, stalking, or intimidating another person.
  • Hate speech — attacks or slurs based on race, ethnicity, religion, nationality, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age, or any protected group.
  • Violence or threats — threatening or encouraging harm to any person.
  • Sexual or explicit content — including nudity and any sexual content involving minors, which we report to the authorities.
  • Illegal activity — promoting or facilitating anything unlawful.
  • Impersonation or fraud — pretending to be someone else, or deceiving people for money or personal data.
  • Spam or scams — repetitive, misleading, or unsolicited promotional content.
  • Private information — sharing someone else's personal details without consent.

Report and block

If you see content or behaviour that breaks these guidelines, report it — every post, comment, message, and profile has a report action in its menu — and block the person so you no longer see them or hear from them. Reports are private and are never shown to the person you reported.

What we do about it

We review every report and act on violations — removing objectionable content and ejecting abusive users within 24 hours. We may suspend or permanently remove accounts that break these guidelines, with or without notice. An automated filter also screens public text as a first line of defence.

If something goes wrong

To report a serious safety concern, or to appeal a decision, contact us at pilatesflowe@gmail.com. We aim to respond promptly.

By using Flowe's community, messaging, and review features you agree to these guidelines. They work alongside Flowe's Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.
